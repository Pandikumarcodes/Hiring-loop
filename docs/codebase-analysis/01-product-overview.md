## VERIFIED

HiringLoop is a multi-tenant applicant tracking and recruiting workspace: jobs and public careers, applications/resumes, interviews/feedback, communication/offers, outcomes, talent pools, analytics and audit. This is code evidence, not runtime certification. ### Feature composition

Organization creation grants Admin membership. Job creation also initializes pipeline and a published form. OPEN jobs are public. Submission binds answers to a published form version, assigns entry stage, associates an S3 resume and appends history. Staff review, schedule, collect feedback, communicate and record outcomes; hiring requires an accepted offer. Selected mutations append audit, while analytics reads operational tables. [hiringloop-backend/src/modules/jobs/repositories/job-repository.js:83](../../hiringloop-backend/src/modules/jobs/repositories/job-repository.js#L83); [hiringloop-backend/src/modules/applications/repositories/application-repository.js:77](../../hiringloop-backend/src/modules/applications/repositories/application-repository.js#L77); [hiringloop-backend/src/modules/applications/use-cases/application-outcome-use-cases.js:1](../../hiringloop-backend/src/modules/applications/use-cases/application-outcome-use-cases.js#L1).
